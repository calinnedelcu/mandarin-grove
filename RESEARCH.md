# WireLingo research

Researched October 1, 2026 using web search and official product pages.

## Exact match found

**WireLingo** (capitalized WireLingo) is the exact product requested. Its language-learning app teaches six languages, including Mandarin, through graded news and stories. There was no need to substitute a similarly named product. [Official site](https://wirelingo.com/)

## Core features

The public site describes graded reading, contextual dictionary lookups, vocabulary and grammar notes, narration, comprehension quizzes, saved-word review, strength scores, spaced repetition, and progress tracking. Additional features include character writing, Sage AI conversations and explanations, and a continuous listening feed called WireWaves. These are the product's advertised capabilities, not features independently tested here. [Features](https://wirelingo.com/features) · [Homepage](https://wirelingo.com/)

## Learning method

WireLingo adapts interesting stories to a learner's level and connects vocabulary to meaning in context. Reading and listening lead into comprehension checks and recurring vocabulary practice. Its explanation invokes comprehensible input: material slightly beyond the learner's current ability. Level matching is guided by CEFR, HSK for Mandarin, and JLPT for Japanese. These are its stated learning principles; this research does not independently establish comparative learning effectiveness. [How it works](https://wirelingo.com/how-it-works)

## UX

The official Android listing describes a feed of graded stories, an adjustable reader, tap-to-define vocabulary, save-word actions, audio speed controls, and quizzes associated with a story. The public pages show reading, quiz, and analytics screens. The intended loop is discover → read/listen → save → practice → track growth. This UX summary comes from public descriptions and screenshots, not a logged-in hands-on evaluation. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wirelingo.app)

## Mandarin Grove design response

Keep the contextual learning loop, but make it approachable for a first-time Mandarin learner: six original everyday micro-readings, 72 HSK 1 starter words, tone-colored hanzi and pinyin, device speech, mixed quizzes, and a simple spaced-review garden. Four thumb-friendly tabs separate Today, Stories, Review, and Words. A calm green palette, rounded cards, animated screen entrances, tactile feedback, XP, and a daily bloom goal make progress visible.

This is an original beginner companion inspired by the method, not a reproduction of WireLingo's content or branding. It uses fixed bundled lessons rather than fresh journalism. There is no AI tutor, cloud account, subscription, or service integration. The dictionary includes a few useful starter expressions such as 这里 and 那里; it is not an official or exhaustive HSK syllabus. Tone labels use dictionary tones; connected speech can change pronunciation through tone sandhi.
